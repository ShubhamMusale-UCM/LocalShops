## 3. Additional Defects Discovered & Fixed During Workflow Verification
- **`OVR-VEL-004` (`shop.service.ts:942`)**: Postgres `"Order"."createdAt"` is `timestamp without time zone` storing UTC; prior SQL `"createdAt" AT TIME ZONE 'Asia/Kolkata'` shifted IST timestamps backward by 11h (`02:50 IST` bucketed into yesterday). Fixed to `("createdAt" AT TIME ZONE 'UTC') AT TIME ZONE 'Asia/Kolkata'`; confirmed `WF2-PLACED` & `WF2-CANCELLED` update `weeklyRevenue[6]` accurately and re-verified all 34 baseline assertions clean.
- **Redis Ads Cache Collision (`ads.service.ts:197` & `767`)**: `getShopAds` (returns array) and `getShopPromotionAnalytics` (returns `{ campaigns, summary }`) shared identical Redis key `shop:${shopId}:ads`, causing `campaigns` on Stats to turn `undefined` for 30s whenever Manage Products ran first. Split into `shop:${shopId}:ads:list` and `shop:${shopId}:ads:analytics`, updated all invalidations across `ads.service.ts`, `ads.controller.ts`, `order.service.ts`, `product.service.ts`, and `telemetrySync.job.ts`, and verified `WF8-ADS-CACHE-ISOLATION` PASS.
